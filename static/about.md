# About Cutie

Cutie makes a **Q**uality **T**est (QT - "Cutie", get it?) of the network, displaying
MOS Quality (a measure of the quality of voice or video call),
Packet Loss, and
Latency/Jitter.
All of these affect your network experience -
the graphs show when performance is bad.
Read more at the
[Github repo](https://github.com/richb-hanover/Cutie-Network-Quality-Test)
