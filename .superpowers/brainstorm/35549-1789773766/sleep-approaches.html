Handling Sleep, Lid Close & Tab Switch

Three approaches to the core problem: browsers freeze JS when a device sleeps or the tab is hidden. Choose the strategy that fits best — or mix them.

The problem
What happens today (no handling)

When the lid closes or screen locks:

  • JS timers freeze — probes stop sending
  • Pending probes all time out at once when it wakes up → spurious loss spike
  • The 2-hour auto-stop timer is also frozen → session runs past 2 hours
  • The "Now" stats panel updates from stale data; charts show a gap
  • On phone: switching tabs produces the same freeze effect
Choose an approach
A

Detect & Pause

When the screen locks / lid closes / tab hides, pause probes and show a yellow "Paused" banner. Resume automatically on wake. Gap is visible in charts as flat line. No data is lost or fabricated.

Pros

  • Session survives sleep
  • Honest gap in charts
  • Auto-resumes

Cons

  • Charts have visible holes
  • Elapsed time includes sleep time
B

Detect & Stop

When the page hides for more than ~5 seconds, stop collection with a specific message: "Paused — lid closed" or "Paused — switched tabs". User clicks Start to begin a new session.

Pros

  • Clean session boundaries
  • No misleading data
  • Simple to understand

Cons

  • Loses data on wake
  • Must click Start again
C

Detect & Annotate

Pause probes on hide, resume on show. Mark the gap on the charts with a shaded region or vertical line labeled "Sleeping 14m" so the viewer can interpret the chart correctly.

Pros

  • Session survives sleep
  • Chart gap is explained
  • Most informative

Cons

  • More complex to build
  • Chart annotation is fiddly
